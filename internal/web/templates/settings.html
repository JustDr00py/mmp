Guide from stream names

Event feeds are often named like Next | Jets @ Patriots | NFL Game Pass | 2026-10-18 | 17:00. The guide then shows “Up next: Jets @ Patriots” until 17:00, the game for three hours, and “Ended” after. Real guides always win.

Group icons

Groups with an icon (set on the Groups page) get an extra category_icon field in get_live_categories. Standard players ignore it and keep showing the name; clients that know it can show the image. M3U playlists are not affected. Turn this off if a player stops listing categories.

Game routing

Slot channels like NBA 06 are renamed for each game, e.g. Milwaukee Bucks vs Toronto Raptors @ Nov 6 8:00 PM :NBA 06. While that game is on, the Bucks and Raptors channels play NBA 06's streams first and their own streams after, then go back to their own list. Their guide stays as it is. Slot streams added to a team channel by hand are taken off it: routing gives them back during the game.
