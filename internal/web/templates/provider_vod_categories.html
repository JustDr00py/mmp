How this works

Titles in other categories are not imported, and their categories don't show up in Movies & series, in bouquets or for customers. Categories the provider adds later stay hidden until you tick them.

Save hides titles outside the chosen categories at once. Save and import also imports the catalog now, which brings in titles from newly ticked categories. Type a language (e.g. English) in the filter, then “tick all shown”, to choose a whole language.
