## M9 — VOD & series pass-through
Movies and series from Xtream providers, re-served to customers through MMP's Xtream API. Pass-through only: nothing is stored or transcoded. Unlike live channels, every viewer gets their own upstream connection (seeking needs it), and that connection holds a provider slot. Hard rules apply as for live: no credentials in logs, and customer URLs point to MMP only.

Xtream client gains `get_vod_categories`, `get_vod_streams`, `get_series_categories`, `get_series`; episodes are fetched lazily with `get_series_info` and cached with a TTL.
